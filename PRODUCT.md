# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors arriving from a portfolio or resume link, evaluating LOCU as a company/product in the making. They spend seconds, not minutes, on this page before deciding whether to look further.

## Product Purpose

A single-page teaser that establishes LOCU's presence ahead of a full product/company launch. Its job is to make a strong, memorable first impression and signal technical craft, not to explain features or convert a sale.

## Positioning

The page itself is the pitch: a real-time WebGL shader interaction (pointer-driven digital distortion with chromatic aberration) standing in place of marketing copy. No other teaser in this portfolio context proves engineering capability through the interface rather than describing it in text.

## Operating Context

- Serves as the centerpiece of a personal portfolio (linked from other portfolio work).
- Hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages — no server, no build pipeline.
- Viewed on both desktop (mouse) and mobile (touch) browsers.
- Single viewport, no scrolling, no navigation — the interaction is the entire page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must compile to plain HTML/CSS/JS — no bundler or build step. CDN-hosted libraries (e.g. Three.js) are acceptable since they require no local build.
- One word, "LOCU," large and centered, is the only content.
- Pointer (desktop) and touch (mobile) both drive the same distortion interaction with equivalent felt quality.
- Page must never scroll, on any viewport size.
- Undecided: whether/when a full LOCU product page follows this teaser.

## Brand Commitments

Name is "LOCU," displayed prominently. No existing logo, color system, or typeface — visual identity is greenfield for this build.

## Evidence on Hand

None provided. No testimonials, customers, product screenshots, or company details exist yet and none should be fabricated or implied by copy.

## Product Principles

1. The craft is the pitch — interaction quality carries the message; no supporting copy is needed or wanted.
2. Radical restraint — one word, no navigation, no scroll; nothing competes with the centerpiece.
3. Machine authenticity — distortion reads as real GPU/shader behavior, not a decorative loop or gif.
4. Parity across input — mouse and touch must feel like the same interaction, not a degraded fallback.
